# Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for OmniCanvas Privacy

**Last Updated:** July 2026

[**OmniCanvas Privacy** - Chrome Web Store](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/cofipknbkehhaildehfcljkedlbpcccp)

Your privacy is extremely important to us. The **OmniCanvas Privacy** browser extension is designed from the ground up to protect your visual privacy while using web platforms. This document explains our privacy practices, permissions, and conditions of use.

---

## PART 1: PRIVACY POLICY

### 1. Collection of Information
**OmniCanvas Privacy does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, browsing history, or sensitive information.** 

All operations performed by the extension happen locally on your device (client-side). We do not operate external servers, and we do not track your usage, messages, prompts, contacts, or analytics.

### 2. Permissions Used and Justification
To function correctly, the extension requires specific permissions within your browser. Here is why they are needed:
*   **`storage`:** Used exclusively to save your preferred privacy mode (Off, Full Blur, Hover, or Smart) and PIN security configuration locally on your device.
*   **Host Permissions:** Required solely to inject the visual blur effects (CSS) and manage the dynamic blur states (JavaScript) on supported web platforms (including communication apps, online email services, and artificial intelligence platforms). The extension cannot read, modify, or harvest your actual message content, credentials, or account data.

### 3. Third-Party Data Sharing and Financial Platforms
Since we do not collect any browser data, **no data is ever sold, traded, or transferred to third parties.**

The extension provides optional links for voluntary financial support through trusted external platforms (**Buy Me a Coffee** and **Livepix**). When you choose to make a donation:
*   Your financial transactions, credit card data, or payment details are processed entirely by those third-party gateways. 
*   OmniCanvas Privacy never sees, handles, or stores your financial or billing information.

### 4. Changes to This Policy
 We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect structural changes in the extension. Any updates will be posted directly on this page with an updated revision date.
